# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Malaysian couples (and their partners/co-planners) organizing a wedding. They use this app daily across mobile and desktop — checking budgets, ticking off checklists, managing guests. Context is personal and high-stakes: a wedding is one of the most significant events in their lives, and the app earns trust by being calm and reliable under pressure.

## Product Purpose

JomKahwin! is a Malay-first, Malaysia-focused wedding planning web app. It centralizes guests, budget, checklist, timetable, suppliers, and countdown in one place. Success means couples feel organized and in control — the anxiety of "have we forgotten something?" is replaced by calm progress visibility. The product is free and approachable, not a premium upsell machine.

## Brand Personality

Modern · Clean · Confident — a trusted planning partner, not a generic form. Feels like a premium Malaysian app built by people who understand local weddings. Direct, never condescending. Warm but not sentimental.

## Anti-references

- Corporate / cold: sterile white, cold blue, enterprise dashboard energy — nothing should feel like an HR tool
- Pastel overload: heavy floral, pink-on-pink, "wedding magazine" excess — not The Knot or WeddingWire
- Generic SaaS cream: Notion-clone aesthetics, template-store layouts, default shadcn without personality

## Design Principles

1. **Calm clarity** — Every screen should reduce anxiety, not add to it. Information is organized, never cluttered.
2. **Malaysian-first** — Copy, tone, and cultural weight reflect local weddings (kenduri, dewan, hantaran). Not a localization of a Western product.
3. **The tool disappears** — UI gets out of the way so couples focus on their wedding, not on figuring out the app.
4. **Confident defaults** — Sensible pre-fills, smart empty states, no "nothing here" dead ends. The app anticipates what you need.
5. **Shared by design** — Features assume both partners are involved. Shared state, shared ownership.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA minimum. High contrast text on all surfaces.
- Mobile-first: many Malaysian users plan on phone, not desktop.
- Bilingual: BM primary, English (UK) secondary — both must feel native, not translated.
- Reduced motion: respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all transitions.
